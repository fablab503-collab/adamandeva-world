Privacy policy

For the website adamandeva.world and the Adamandeva apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch. Last updated 27 September 2026.

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We do not collect personal data, and we do not sell or share any.

The apps

The apps download the stories from adamandeva.world and nothing else. They do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or anything on your device.

Your settings and the stories you save are kept on your own device. A copy of the stories is kept there too, so you can read offline. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it all, and Settings has buttons to clear it earlier.

The World map uses Apple Maps. Apple's own privacy policy covers the map.

If you open a story's source link, the newsroom's own site opens, with its own privacy rules.

The website

adamandeva.world is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps visitors' IP addresses in its logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Your browser loads the fonts from Google Fonts. The World map on the website loads its tiles from OpenFreeMap and, depending on what you open there, data from OpenStreetMap search, Open-Meteo, USGS, the World Bank and Wikipedia. Each of them sees your IP address when that happens.

The page and the map may keep a few settings in your own browser. They are never sent to us.

Children

Nothing here is aimed at children, and we collect no data from anyone, children included.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date. Questions: see Support.
